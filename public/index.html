How to watch

  • Scroll / pinch zoom from the whole continent down to a single person
  • Left-drag move around · Right-drag rotate & tilt
  • Click a kingdom (castle, name or realm list) to watch it as a movie; press ⚡ God mode to return
  • Click a person to inspect them · Double-click the ground to fly there
  • Turn on 🔇 Sound for wind, birds, rain, battles, voices and the narrator
  • Click the minimap to jump anywhere
  • Nobody is scripted: every person decides for themselves when to work, eat, drink, marry, flee, fight or rebel. The weather is random.
